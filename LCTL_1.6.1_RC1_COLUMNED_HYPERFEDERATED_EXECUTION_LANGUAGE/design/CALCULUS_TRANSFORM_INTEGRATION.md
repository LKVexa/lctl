# Calculus Transform Integration Architecture

The Calculus Transform is used as a mathematical guide for LCTL's continuum and approximation layers. The core translation rules are typed transforms rather than global substitutions.

1. **Operator preservation:** finite coupling/operator structures can acquire integral/Fredholm or elliptic generator images only under named hypotheses.
2. **Variational semantics:** quadratic forms, optimization, and stability claims may acquire Dirichlet-energy, Euler–Lagrange, Rayleigh, or KKT representations.
3. **Differentiation honesty:** local linearization uses D13 and carries the explicit second-order remainder envelope.
4. **Retraction:** transformed semantics must carry a Q5 replay/retraction path; Θ-J's lattice retraction and Θ-G's bandwidth error are recorded rather than erased.
5. **Obstruction preservation:** D14 is a first-class rejection route.
6. **Quantum guardrail:** diffusion/Fokker–Planck/heat-flow semantics are confined to classical stochastic, control, environment, or continuum approximation layers. Closed quantum systems remain unitary/Hamiltonian; open quantum systems use density operators and CPTP channels.

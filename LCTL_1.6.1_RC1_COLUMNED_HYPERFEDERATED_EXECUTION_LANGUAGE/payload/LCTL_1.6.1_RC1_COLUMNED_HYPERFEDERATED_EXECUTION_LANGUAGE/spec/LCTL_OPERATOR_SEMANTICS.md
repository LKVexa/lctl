# Operator Semantics

Primitive exact operators include I, X, Y, Z, H, S, S†, T, T†, RX, RY, RZ, phase, CX/CNOT, CY, CZ, CH, SWAP, iSWAP, controlled phase and CCX. Unary operators act on a declared qubit; controlled operators explicitly name control and target. Exact source operators are validated and simulated as complex linear maps. Custom matrix assertions test unitarity/Hermiticity/projector properties.

# LCTL Calculus Transform Profile (LCTL-CT)

This profile uses the attached *Foundations of the Calculus Transform* as a mathematical guide, not as a replacement for quantum mechanics. Its governing discipline is: a discrete object may receive a continuum image only with named regularity, a quantified limit/error statement, and a Q5 retraction.

Admitted transform operators: D1 Fredholm/generator; D2 spectral/Sturm-Liouville/Rayleigh; D3 Dirichlet energy; D4 Hilbert projection/Gram volume; D5 kernel/index; D6 exact log differential; D7 bifurcation/implicit-function threshold; D8 heat semigroup/Fourier symbol; D9 geodesic/eikonal; D10 Fokker-Planck/Wasserstein flow for classical/stochastic density models; D11 variational Euler-Lagrange/KKT; D12 convolution/Fourier symbol; D13 Frechet linearization with second-order remainder; D14 obstruction/vanishing first variation; Theta-G continuum generator; Theta-J lattice/continuum retraction.

Quantum guardrail: unitary closed-system evolution remains unitary/Hamiltonian quantum dynamics. Diffusion/Fokker-Planck images are used only for explicitly classical stochastic, environment, control, or continuum-approximation models. A transform row that would conflate these regimes is rejected semantically by specification even when syntactically representable.

Retraction contract: `S_l o T_cal = T_8S + O(l^2)` under the transform hypotheses, with the additional Theta-G bandwidth error where the weighted Laplacian limit is used. LCTL records both lattice scale and bandwidth error instead of erasing them.
